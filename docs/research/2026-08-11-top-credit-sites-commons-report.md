## What they all have in common

### 1. Outcome language, not feature dumps
Lead with what credit *unlocks* (rates, approvals, housing, options) — not “we send letters.”

### 2. Free / low-friction first step
Universal: free score, free monitor, free consult, free analysis. Paid sits behind trust.

### 3. Build · Repair · Monitor mental model
Even if a brand only does one, consumers arrive expecting all three pathways labeled clearly.

### 4. Trust + legitimacy scaffolding
Security language, bureau names, clear pricing or “how we get paid,” process steps, accessible FAQs. YMYL: Google rewards education + transparency over hype.

### 5. Score as hero visual vernacular
Dials / gauges / number callouts. Always paired (or should be) with “illustrative / not a guarantee.”

### 6. Benefit grids for high scores
Lower rates, better odds, housing leverage, insurance effects (where true), peace of mind.

### 7. Mobile-first, persistent CTA
Sticky header CTA; one primary action per viewport.

### 8. Color = trust + growth
- Monitoring leaders: green optimism (Credit Karma) or blue trust (Experian, myFICO + warm accent).
- Repair leaders: navy / professional blues, high-contrast CTAs.
- Pattern to steal: **dark authority + single growth accent + cool light surfaces.**

### 9. Education hubs
Score factors, dispute rights, how monitoring works — builds SEO and trust under YMYL.

### 10. Disclaimers near claims
Results vary; not a lender; scores from different models differ; you can dispute for free yourself.

### 11. Separate personal vs (when offered) business
Business credit is almost never mixed into the personal home hero — own nav / own page.

### 12. Compliance landmines (repair category)
Leaders who lasted: no score guarantees, no “delete anything,” no illegal advance fees, clear cancellation rights. 2025 Lexington / CreditRepair.com enforcement = cautionary tale.

## Color / UI recommendations (implemented)

- **Navy authority** (deeper than Experian cobalt) — “Power”
- **Emerald ascent** (growth without Karma lime clone)
- **Cloud `#F7F9FC`** cool white (not cream AI-default)
- **Gold `#C6A75E`** only for milestones / plan accents
- Distinctive type (not Inter): display personality + highly legible body
- 2–3 intentional motions (hero entrance, scroll reveal, CTA hover) — not particle noise

## Conversion architecture (shared winners)

1. Above-fold benefit thesis + single CTA  
2. Social / process trust before ask  
3. Short form or calendar (not 20 fields)  
4. Consent + disclaimer near form  
5. Clear “what happens next”

**Our primary CTA:** Book Free Consultation (Calendly).
